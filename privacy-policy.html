Privacy Policy

Published: August 28, 2026

1. General

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") describes how Insta-Reply (the "Service") collects, uses, and protects data when you connect your Instagram account via the Instagram Graph API. The Service automatically replies to comments on your posts and sends direct messages to commenters.

By connecting your Instagram account to the Service, you agree to the terms of this Policy.

2. Legal basis for processing

Data is processed based on your explicit consent, given when you authorize (via OAuth) your Instagram/Facebook account to connect to the Service. Connecting your account constitutes this consent.

3. What data we collect

Through the Instagram Graph API, and only after you connect your account, the Service accesses:

  • the ID and access token of your connected Instagram Business account;
  • the text and ID of comments left on posts of your connected account, including the commenter's ID;
  • the technical ability to reply to comments and send direct messages on behalf of your connected account.

We do not collect passwords, payment information, or any other personal data unrelated to the auto-reply functionality.

4. How we use your data

Data is used exclusively to:

  • automatically generate and post a reply to a comment on your post;
  • automatically send a direct message to the comment author;
  • display usage statistics to you (e.g., number of comments processed).

We do not use your data for advertising or marketing purposes, and we do not share it with third parties, except where required by applicable law.

5. Data storage and security

Access tokens and related settings are stored securely on the servers used to run the Service. Access to this data is restricted to the purposes described in Section 4.

6. Your rights

You may, at any time:

  • revoke the Service's access to your Instagram account via Instagram/Facebook settings (Settings → Apps and Websites → Insta-Reply → Remove Access);
  • request information about what data is being processed;
  • request full deletion of your data.

7. Data deletion

Once you revoke the Service's access via Instagram/Facebook settings, we stop processing data associated with that account.

To request full deletion of already-stored data (including access tokens and comment history), email info@instareply.ru with your Instagram account name. Your data will be deleted within 7 business days of receiving the request.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this address, with the publication date shown above.

9. Contact

For any questions about this Policy, contact: info@instareply.ru
